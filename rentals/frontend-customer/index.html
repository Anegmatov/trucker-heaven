Questions

What do I need to bring?

A valid driver license, proof of insurance, and a card or cash for the rental and the refundable deposit. You sign the rental agreement at the counter.

When do I get the deposit back?

When you return the car and we've checked it in. Cash deposits are returned at the counter; card deposits are released back to your card.

Is anything charged online?

No. Reserving online only holds the car for you. You pay at the counter when you pick up.

Can I cancel?

Yes, online from your reservation page, any time before pickup. Nothing has been charged, so there's nothing to refund.

What if I return it late or need it longer?

Call the counter. We'll extend it if the car is free, and settle any extra days when you return.

Where do I find my reservation?

Use the link in your confirmation text, or go to My rental and enter the email and phone you reserved with.

# Section classifications

`parking_spots` persists physical section, configured parking use, public-bookable flag, classification state, and operational label. For Trucker Heaven, `TRACTOR_TRAILER` remains the compatibility enum for the 86 General Truck spaces (111–196), but customer and admin presentation calls it General Truck Parking. Bobtail 10–80 and 197–204 are public; Shop 81–90 and Company 101–110 are internal. No Trucker Only or Trailer Only space is assigned. Operational status remains separately derived from inactive, block, assignment, and reservation state.
